A tiny light-up screen that keeps getting new pictures.

Your Pocket Jumbotron plays bright, colorful animations, your own pictures, messages, a clock, countdowns, the weather and fireworks. You change it from your phone over your home WiFi.

What it does

An 8,192-light screen in a frame, with a small computer on the back.

🎞️

Your own pictures

Photos, GIFs and animated pictures from your phone. They're resized to fit on the phone, you see a preview, then they're sent to the screen.

🌈

Much richer color

Smooth colors and gradients, and a full-color mode for photos. The original software could only show 7 colors.

💬

Messages, clock & countdowns

“Happy Birthday!” in big letters, the time, or “12 days until Christmas”, with snow, hearts, confetti or fireworks behind them.

⛅

Weather & effects

Today's weather for your town, and fireworks, fire, starfield and more, drawn live by the screen.

🎁

New picture packs

One tap on the screen's page gets the latest Pocket Jumbotron pictures. Some families also get a code for their own private pictures.

🌙

Night mode

Dims or switches off the screen at night, at the hours you pick.

Getting started

After the one-time install, everything happens on your phone over your home WiFi.

Once

Put it on your WiFi

  1. Hold the button on the back for 2 seconds.
  2. The screen shows a WiFi name (PocketJumbo- and 4 letters) and a password. Join it on your phone.
  3. A page opens by itself. Pick your home WiFi and type its password.
  4. The screen restarts and joins your WiFi.
Any time

Change what it shows

  1. Press the button once. The screen shows a QR code.
  2. Point your phone's camera at it (or open Open my screen).
  3. Add pictures, messages, the clock, weather or effects, in any order.
  4. Tap Send to screen. Your new show replaces the old one.
Switching over takes one visit to a computer. Your screen needs a one-time install from Chrome or Edge with a USB cable. After that, updates and new pictures arrive over WiFi. Install the new software →

Questions

Do I have to put it on my WiFi?

No. Without WiFi it keeps playing whatever is on it. To change the pictures, the clock or the weather it needs your home WiFi.

My phone can't open the screen's page.

Your phone must be on the same WiFi as the screen. On Android, Chrome may ask to allow finding devices on your network ("Nearby devices"): allow it. Press the screen's button to see its address.

Can an update break it?

No. It keeps the old software until the new version has started up properly. If something goes wrong, it switches back on its own.

Will the old HD2020 / LedArt apps still work?

No. After the switch, the Pocket Jumbotron page on your phone replaces those apps.

How much can it hold?

About 4 MB: a few dozen animations, fewer full-color ones. The page shows how much space your show needs. Messages, the clock, weather and effects take no space. Sending a show replaces the old one, so it never runs out of space.

Why isn't it full brightness?

It's set a little below maximum on purpose. It's still easy to see, and it keeps the power plug and supply cool.

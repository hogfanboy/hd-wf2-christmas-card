A tiny light-up screen that keeps getting new pictures.

Your Pocket Jumbotron plays bright, colorful animations. It can pick up fresh ones all season long, or show photos and videos you add yourself.

What it does

An 8,192-light screen in a frame, with a small computer on the back.

🎞️

Plays animations

It loops through a playlist of animations and pictures, all day, on its own.

🌈

Much richer color

The new software shows smooth colors and gradients. The original software couldn't do that.

📸

Your own photos & videos

Pick something on your phone. It gets resized to fit, you see a preview, then it's sent to the screen.

🎁

Fresh content all season

When it's on your WiFi, it can download a new batch of animations every day through the holidays.

Two ways to get new pictures

Choose whichever suits you. Connecting to your home WiFi is optional.

Automatic

Over your home WiFi

Set it up once and it takes care of itself.

  1. Press the button on the back.
  2. On your phone, join the WiFi named PocketJumbotron.
  3. Choose your home WiFi and type its password.
  4. That's it. Once a day it checks for new animations and updates.
No WiFi needed

Straight from your phone

It never connects to your home network or the internet.

  1. Press the button on the back.
  2. On your phone, join the WiFi named PocketJumbotron.
  3. A page opens. Add photos, GIFs or a short video.
  4. Tap Send. The new pictures replace the old ones.

Questions

Do I have to put it on my WiFi?

No. Without WiFi, it keeps playing whatever is on it, and you can add new pictures straight from your phone whenever you want.

Can an update break it?

No. It keeps the old software until the new version has started up properly. If something goes wrong, it switches back on its own.

Will the old HD2020 / LedArt apps still work?

No. After the switch, the Pocket Jumbotron page on your phone replaces those apps.

How much can it hold?

About a dozen animations, or roughly a minute of video. Each new batch replaces the previous one, so it never runs out of space.

Why isn't it full brightness?

It's set a little below maximum on purpose. It's still easy to see, and it keeps the power plug and supply cool.

Switch your screen to the new software

A one-time job, about 2 minutes. After this, updates and new pictures arrive over WiFi. Already switched and only need new WiFi? Change the WiFi only.

This needs Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac or Chromebook). Phones and Safari/Firefox can't talk to the screen over USB.
Open this page there:
You need: your screen, the USB cable with the same flat plug on both ends (USB-A to USB-A), and a computer with Chrome or Edge.
Computer only has the small oval USB-C ports? A USB-C to USB-A adapter works fine.
Step 1 of 2 · Install the software
  1. Unplug the screen's power supply (the round plug on the back). The panel can stay connected.
  2. Plug the USB cable into the blue board first, in the USB socket next to the two screws. That end is the tight one, so get it done now. Leave the other end unplugged.
  3. Click the yellow button just below. A small window opens at the top of the browser. It says "No compatible devices found." That's normal: the screen isn't plugged into the computer yet. Leave that window open (don't click Cancel) and go on to step 4.
Nothing shows up in the window?
  • Unplug the cable from the computer, hold S1 and plug it in again. Without S1 held, the screen only shows up for a moment.
  • Plug straight into the computer, not into a USB hub or keyboard, and try another USB port.
  • Some cables can only charge. Try another USB-A to USB-A cable.
  • Windows, the first time: give it a few seconds to set up the USB connection.
  • Still nothing? Close the window, reload this page and start again at step 3. Trying again can't hurt the screen.
  1. Press and hold S1, the small button on the top edge of the blue board, and still holding it, plug the other end of the cable into the computer.
  2. Within a second or two "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" appears in the window. Click it, then click Connect. Keep holding the button.
  3. Let go of the button when it says Installing, and wait for Done. Don't unplug anything while it works.
Step 1 is done. Don't unplug yet! Now step 2: connect the screen to your home WiFi, just below. Leave the USB cable plugged in for it. ↓

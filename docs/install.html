Switch your screen to the new software

A one-time job, about 2 minutes. After this, updates and new pictures arrive over WiFi. Already switched and only need new WiFi? Change the WiFi only.

This needs Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac or Chromebook). Phones and Safari/Firefox can't talk to the screen over USB.
You need: your screen, the USB cable with the same flat plug on both ends (USB-A to USB-A), and a computer with Chrome or Edge.
Step 1 of 2 · Install the software
  1. Unplug the screen's power supply. Then unplug the two cables going to the LED panel (the flat data ribbon and the power lead) so only the small controller board is left. The computer only has to power that board.
  2. Plug the USB cable into the controller board first. That end is the tight one, so get it done now. Leave the other end unplugged.
  3. Click the yellow button below. A small window opens listing devices. It's empty for now; leave it open.
  4. Press and hold the button on the board (marked S1) and, still holding it, plug the other end of the cable into the computer.
  5. Within a second or two "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" appears in the window. Click it, then click Connect. Keep holding the button.
  6. Let go of the button when it says Installing, and wait for Done. Don't unplug anything while it works.
Step 1 is done. Don't unplug yet! Now step 2: connect the screen to your home WiFi, just below. Leave the USB cable plugged in for it. ↓
